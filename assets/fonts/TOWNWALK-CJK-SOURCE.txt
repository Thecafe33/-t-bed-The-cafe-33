Townwalk SC is a renamed horizontal-text subset of Noto Sans CJK SC Regular/Bold.
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
Font licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see noto-cjk-LICENSE.
The original copyright and licence metadata remain in the font files.
The subset covers Chinese characters in the shipped interface and translations.
Build: python3 tools/subset_cjk_font.py --source-dir /path/to/noto/ttc
Requires Python fontTools and brotli. TTC font index 2 is Simplified Chinese.
